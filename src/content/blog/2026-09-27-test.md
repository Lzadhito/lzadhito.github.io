I've been trying on two characters lately: First, the only speak when it matters. Second, the speak out (too) loud thinking. 

While the first one is ideal, but if you're like me then you might have difficulties to connect all the dots without creating statements on it. 

There are a lot of risks for being the second. As we know, we might distracting the discussions  with our unconnected minds. And the new thing that I'm figuring out recently is being ignored. It requires you to bear the risk of being ignored. This is the hardest one, ngl. But I think it would worth the effort.

Being okay to be ignored would help you numerous things: you could be more invincible (or "unpredictable" in another word), helps you to be more sincere and truthfull when doing good deed, and etc. you tell me.
