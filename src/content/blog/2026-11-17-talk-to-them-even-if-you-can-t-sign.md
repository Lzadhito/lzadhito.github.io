I befriended a deaf couple, and numbers of deaf people this month. It was a whole new world that I my foot every stepepd, made me realize such a grateful life I ever had in my life. Even compared to the trauma, the problems, the time when I felt I was the most tormented (admit it, everyone been there 😂), the life they lived with the struggles, what they've been through not even close to mine.

Mister Edwin is a deaf from birth, the eldest from his two normal siblings. He born from a very rich dad, a high profile judge. He signed  "my father is TOP! he had 6 cars and 2 bikes when he was young" (from his age, I believe having a bike at that time is very prestigious, moreover cars!). He live in a big house in South Jakarta (the most expensive land on earth after California). Then, his father slowly suffer an illness (I don't know what it is, I can't fully understand the hand sign either), he signed that his father often work overnight and sleepless until it make him sick. The cars sold one by one to fund the treatment. The old man had a surgery in his chest (it probably a blood pressure, or a heart issue). Until he passed away, all the cars are sold, and so does his mansion. He now lived in Sawangan (affordable land at that time, with the cost of 24/7 traffic jam), selling 10/10 Mie Ayam in Cinere only in 15k (compared to Bakmie Bangka there that cost 35k that I would probably "cuih" at for such price) that I absolutely fell in love with makes me no more mie ayam besides his. It only target 20pcs every day which roughly about 9mio per month for a couple around 50s. I hope it draw clearly how he downscale his lifestyle significantly.

After hearing this story you know what I felt? Its not "well, at least he was having rich family", it is about (probably) how he felt "how such a burden am I" , he lived in very rich env and it slowly broke because their dependency of his father and he, as a man with disability could not probably met his dad capacity to retain how they live daily. As he is a disable, I'm absolutely sure that he would felt sorry all the time. To be honest, after that chat I just paid my Mie Ayam, say goodbye, and go back home, I cannot stand his story.

I've seen other deaf person who attempt suicide just because the guilty feelings. But this man, is a living proof of a person who live in that shoe longer that I ever lived. I know that guilty feeling could even make normal people hoping to not waking up anymore. I even have a friend  who nearly committing suicide because of an internal pressure and when he told me that he will do it, I said "go ahead" (If you're reading this, I'm sorry dude, I really am and I really proud of what you become now, happy with your life and our common 9to5 struggles & midlife crisis now), but when this deaf man would commit it, I would pray that I have a ben10 watch and transform to Canonbolt just to save him when he tryna jump off a building.

Trust me, their shoes harder than Docmart boots that scratch your toe everytime you use public transport. They really loved to be talked with, even when you cannot do a single hand sign. They love it even when they could only read your lips, or even read your meaningless hand swing tryna make a hand sign. If you see any of them, pleas talk to them.
